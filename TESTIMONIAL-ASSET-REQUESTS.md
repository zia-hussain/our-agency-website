# Testimonial / Proof Asset Requests

Generated during the Phase 1 truth & proof foundation pass. These are the specific gaps where real client proof would materially improve conversion — not a request for every review on file. Don't fill these with generic testimonial copy or placeholders; leave the surfaces as they are until real assets arrive.

---

### 1. Learning Platform SaaS — stabilization trust

**Page / section:** Wherever the Learning Platform SaaS case study surfaces (its own page now; potentially homepage later).
**Buyer question:** "Will these people actually take ownership of something that's already broken, not just build shiny new things?"
**Ideal proof:** The client describing the stabilization experience specifically — being trusted with an existing, messy product; follow-through; clarity brought to the chaos.
**Format:** Written quote preferred, given the anonymity constraint (2–3 sentences). Video only if the client is willing to appear on camera without naming the product.
**Length:** 2–3 sentences (written) or 20–30 sec (video).
**Client type:** The Learning Platform SaaS founder, or an equivalent "product rescue" client if this one isn't available.

---

### 2. Knipsr & Liftly — did it ship, did it hold up

**Page / section:** Knipsr and Liftly case study pages.
**Buyer question:** "Did this actually ship and work in the real world, or is this just a technology list?"
**Ideal proof:** One short confirming line from each founder about the outcome — reliability for Knipsr, the V1/V2 sequencing decision paying off for Liftly.
**Format:** Written, 1–2 sentences each. Identity can stay first-name-only or fully anonymous per the current disclosure rules for these two.
**Length:** 1–2 sentences.
**Client type:** The Knipsr founder and the Liftly founder specifically.

---

### 3. Homepage — a trust format beyond text

**Page / section:** Homepage, after the flagship case studies.
**Buyer question:** "I would be comfortable showing them my actual problem" — this is a felt-trust question that a video answers better than another paragraph of text.
**Ideal proof:** A short video from any flagship client (Forlag, Floating Stone Ranch, Skill x Swap, or a newly cleared one) speaking to working style, responsiveness, or follow-through — not a scripted ad.
**Format:** Video preferred; written fallback if video isn't available.
**Length:** 20–40 seconds.
**Client type:** Any flagship client comfortable being filmed.

---

### 4. Marketplace rating — give the number some substance

**Page / section:** Wherever the Upwork "Top Rated" badge appears (Hero, Footer, About, Portfolio stats).
**Buyer question:** "Is 100% Job Success just a number, or does real client feedback back it up?"
**Ideal proof:** One or two of the strongest verbatim marketplace reviews (Upwork/Fiverr), quotable with permission, to sit near the badge.
**Format:** Written excerpt, short.
**Length:** 1–2 sentences each.
**Client type:** Any marketplace client with a strong review already on file.

---
